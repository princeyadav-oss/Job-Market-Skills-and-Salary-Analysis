# 📊 Job Market Skills and Salary Analysis

> **Turning job-market data into meaningful insights about skills, salaries, experience, job roles, and locations.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Job Market Skills and Salary Analysis** project focuses on analyzing job-market data to understand important patterns in **salary, job demand, technical skills, experience levels, companies, and locations**.

Using **Python, Pandas, NumPy, and interactive Plotly visualizations**, the project transforms raw job-market data into clear and useful insights.

The analysis can help **students, job seekers, and aspiring professionals** understand current job-market trends and make better career and skill-development decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze salary patterns across different job roles.
- Identify high-demand job roles and technical skills.
- Compare salaries across different experience levels.
- Analyze location-wise job and salary trends.
- Study the relationship between technical skills and salary.
- Analyze the relationship between experience, skill count, and salary.
- Explore company and employment-type trends.
- Present insights using interactive and animated visualizations.
- Provide useful insights for career and skill-development decisions.
